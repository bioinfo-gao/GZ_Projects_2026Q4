========================================================================================
TASK — glioma_margin_rate_limit_v4
========================================================================================

DATASET: https://github.com/bioinformatics-gao/CASE-BBB-prediction-Data

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUESTION (this is the prompt given to the model)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A patient has recurrent glioblastoma. Three cytotoxic agents are under consideration.
Their blood-brain barrier (BBB) permeability labels are recorded in the accompanying
dataset (`1) Supplementary-data/TableS1-Drug-for-Train-and-Validation.xlsx`, column `BBB
+/-`):

  * lomustine — dataset label `Y`
  * methotrexate — dataset label `N`
  * doxorubicin — dataset label `N`

Bevacizumab (anti-VEGF) is under consideration in addition to cytotoxic therapy. Where a
question concerns anti-VEGF therapy, answer for sustained therapy, beyond any transient
vascular-normalisation window.

Write your answer as JSON to `eval_answer.json` with exactly these fields:

  * `primary_determinant_of_margin_response` (string) — one of
    `bbb_permeability_of_the_agent`, `proliferative_state_of_the_target_cells`,
    `interstitial_transport_beyond_the_vessel_wall`,
    `agent_specific_dna_repair_capacity`, `immunosuppressive_microenvironment`. The
    factor that primarily determines whether these agents reduce the cell population at
    the infiltrating tumour margin.
  * `agent_selection_by_bbb_label` (string) — one of
    `lomustine_preferred_and_this_predicts_response`,
    `lomustine_preferred_but_this_does_not_predict_response`,
    `no_label_based_preference_exists`,
    `methotrexate_preferred_and_this_predicts_response`. How the dataset `BBB +/-`
    labels bear on the choice among the three agents for this patient.
  * `antivegf_effect_on_margin_exposure` (string) — one of `increases`, `decreases`,
    `unchanged`. The effect of sustained anti-VEGF therapy on cytotoxic drug exposure of
    the infiltrating tumour margin.
  * `antivegf_effect_on_margin_cytotoxic_sensitivity` (string) — one of `increases`,
    `decreases`, `no_effect`. The effect of sustained anti-VEGF therapy on the intrinsic
    sensitivity of the infiltrating margin cell population to these three agents,
    setting aside any change in the amount of drug delivered.
  * `relapse_pattern_after_antivegf` (string) — one of `enhancing_and_local`,
    `non_enhancing_and_distant`, `unchanged_from_baseline_pattern`. The characteristic
    radiographic pattern of relapse on sustained anti-VEGF therapy.

All fields are categorical. Report exactly one option per field.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CORRECT ANSWER
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  primary_determinant_of_margin_response           =  proliferative_state_of_the_target_cells
  agent_selection_by_bbb_label                     =  lomustine_preferred_but_this_does_not_predict_response
  antivegf_effect_on_margin_exposure               =  decreases
  antivegf_effect_on_margin_cytotoxic_sensitivity  =  decreases
  relapse_pattern_after_antivegf                   =  non_enhancing_and_distant

  (grader: MultipleChoice; 5 fields; every tolerance is zero / exact match)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE TRAP
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The decisive pairing is `primary_determinant_of_margin_response` against
`agent_selection_by_bbb_label`. An agent can read the table correctly - lomustine is the
only `Y` - and still be wrong, because the question is whether that reading predicts
response. The correct answer concedes the label-based preference and denies its
predictive value, which is the one combination requiring both halves of the picture.
`no_label_based_preference_exists` is wrong on the data: the labels do differ, and
lomustine is the one that crosses an intact barrier.

The reasoning behind the denial, none of which is stated in the task: the margin niche
is hypoxic; hypoxia suppresses cycling there; and all three agents are replication-
contingent, methotrexate as an S-phase-specific antifolate, doxorubicin because
topoisomerase II-DNA complexes become lethal double-strand breaks during replication,
and the nitrosourea because its crosslinks are converted into lethal lesions principally
during replication. A cell that is not cycling therefore does not transduce any of these
drug-target interactions into death, however much drug arrives.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS IS HARD / DISTRACTORS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Four independent traps.

First, the BBB decoy. The accompanying dataset is a BBB permeability dataset and the
labels are the only agent-level information given, so an agent answering
`bbb_permeability_of_the_agent` is not pattern-matching carelessly - it is reasoning
from the only evidence in front of it. The option list offers no elimination path: all
five options are properties of the tumour or of the delivery problem rather than of any
one drug, so the shared-property shortcut that solved an earlier version of this task
does not apply. `interstitial_transport_beyond_the_vessel_wall` and
`immunosuppressive_microenvironment` are both real and heavily cited;
`agent_specific_dna_repair_capacity` is the MGMT answer in general form, which is the
single most-cited resistance mechanism in this disease.

Second and third, the two anti-VEGF direction fields. The published rationale for adding
anti-VEGF therapy to cytotoxics and radiation is that normalisation improves perfusion
and oxygenation and thereby both delivery and sensitivity. Under sustained therapy the
barrier is restored instead, which reduces access to the non-enhancing margin, and
vessel pruning deepens margin hypoxia, which suppresses cycling further and so reduces
sensitivity to three replication-contingent agents. Both correct answers are therefore
the opposite of the dominant published frame, and both option sets are bare directions,
so neither can be reached by recognising mechanism vocabulary.

Fourth, `relapse_pattern_after_antivegf` is a coherence check. An agent that answered
the two direction fields in the normalisation direction and then answers
`non_enhancing_and_distant` here has stated a relapse pattern its own mechanism cannot
produce. Scoring the fields jointly separates an agent that holds a mechanism from one
that retrieves facts.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS TESTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Whether the agent knows that the most salient variable in the data it is given is not
the operative one, without being told so.

The prompt supplies a BBB permeability dataset and nothing but BBB labels for the three
agents, so every available cue points at BBB crossing as the decision variable. In
glioblastoma it largely is not: the contrast-enhancing bulk has already disrupted its
own barrier, so an agent's intact-BBB permeability does not determine exposure there,
and at the infiltrating margin, where the barrier is relatively preserved, exposure
still does not determine kill because the target cells are largely not cycling. Both
halves of that are withheld from the prompt. An agent that does not hold them will
reason correctly from the labels it was given and arrive at a confident, data-grounded,
wrong recommendation: prefer lomustine, and expect the preference to translate into
response.

The second half tests whether the agent applies the glioblastoma observations for
sustained anti-VEGF therapy or the direction asserted by the vascular-normalisation
literature. These two bodies of work disagree, and which one an agent applies to this
disease is the benchmark-relevant question.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW THE GROUND TRUTH WAS DERIVED
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each field traces to a documented statement rather than to a calculation. Dataset labels
for the three agents were read directly from TableS1 (lomustine Y, methotrexate N,
doxorubicin N; verified 2026-11-05). Barrier disruption in the contrast-enhancing bulk
versus relative integrity at the non-enhancing infiltrating margin, and the margin as
the origin of recurrence, are drawn from the glioblastoma drug-delivery literature.
Replication dependence of alkylator cytotoxicity via the O6-methylguanine mismatch-
repair futile cycle, and the requirement that lesions pass through a replication cycle
to become lethal, are established mechanistically. Hypoxic maintenance of a non-cycling
stem-like fraction and its association with therapeutic resistance and recurrence are
likewise documented. Anti-VEGF restoration of barrier integrity with reduced
enhancement, the characteristically non-enhancing and distant relapse pattern that
follows, and vessel pruning with deepened tumour hypoxia under sustained therapy, are
the reported glioblastoma observations. See docs/P48_research_plan_and_review_1105.md
for the per-claim sourcing and for the claims deliberately not made.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GRADING
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MultipleChoice across five closed option sets. No numeric field, therefore no tolerance
to calibrate - the documented failure mode of numeric graders in this benchmark family
is avoided by construction. Partial credit is informative: the first two fields test
whether the agent knows which variable governs the outcome, the next two test whether it
holds the direction of the anti-VEGF effect, and the last is a coherence check on the
two direction fields.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DATA / PLATFORM
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Reasoning task over drug-level tabular labels. No computation, no randomness, no
reference genome. Shares its dataset with bbb_label_implication_v1, which is its
deliberate counterpart: that task requires the BBB labels to be taken seriously as data,
this one requires recognising that they do not govern the clinical outcome.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LIMITATIONS AND KNOWN GAPS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Four gaps. None changes an answer; all of them bound the claims.

A transient vascular-normalisation window during which perfusion and oxygenation do
improve is documented. The task is therefore explicitly scoped to sustained therapy
beyond that window. Without that scoping, `increases` would be defensible on both
direction fields and the item would be ambiguous rather than hard. The scoping sentence
reveals that such a window exists, which is a deliberate cost paid for determinacy; it
does not reveal the direction outside it.

Deepened hypoxia under sustained anti-VEGF therapy rests more heavily on preclinical
models than on direct human measurement at the infiltrating margin; the clinical
inference runs through the relapse phenotype rather than through oxygen measurement in
margin tissue. `decreases` is the defensible direction, but it is an inference, not a
measurement.

No retrieved source directly measured sparing of non-cycling glioma cells by these three
specific agents. That step is an inference from the established replication requirement,
which is why the reasoning is framed in terms of replication dependence rather than an
asserted sparing fraction. One study in Xenopus extracts proposes a replication-
independent component of alkylator action operating alongside the cell-cycle-dependent
mode, which would attenuate but not reverse the conclusion.

Because the agents' mechanisms are no longer stated, an agent that does not know that
methotrexate is an S-phase-specific antifolate, or that doxorubicin's lethality requires
replication, fails for want of standard pharmacology rather than for want of the
intended insight. These three agents were chosen partly because that pharmacology is not
obscure, but the failure mode is real and model results should be read with it in mind.

Temozolomide is deliberately absent: it is the standard-of-care agent and the clearest
example of replication-dependent cytotoxicity, but it is not present in the dataset, so
including it would have meant asserting a BBB label the data does not carry.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPECIFICATION PRINCIPLE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This task states what varies between patients and cannot be inferred - the diagnosis,
which three agents are under consideration, their labels as the dataset records them,
that anti-VEGF therapy is also under consideration, and the temporal scope of the anti-
VEGF questions. It states no disease biology. Barrier disruption in the contrast-
enhancing bulk, relative barrier integrity at the non-enhancing margin, the margin as
the origin of recurrence, the hypoxic niche, and the mechanism of each of the three
agents are all general properties of glioblastoma or standard pharmacology, and all of
them are withheld, because supplying them is supplying the answer. The question is
specified exhaustively; the biology is not. Earlier versions of this task conflated the
two and stated the biology in the premises, which is the single error they share.
